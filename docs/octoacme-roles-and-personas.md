# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers ensure that solutions are usable, accessible, and grounded in customer needs. They turn research findings into workflows, prototypes, and design guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research
- Create user flows, prototypes, and visual design specifications
- Identify usability and accessibility risks
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user needs to product goals and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm design feasibility
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility checks in validation
- Share research findings and design decisions with Project Managers for planning and risk tracking

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction and help the team make consistent, sustainable design decisions. They own or facilitate technical decisions without replacing the implementation responsibilities of Developers.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical approaches and architecture constraints
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Facilitate design reviews and document significant decisions
- Support estimation, sequencing, and technical trade-off analysis

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advise Project Managers on technical dependencies, risks, and delivery sequencing
- Work with Product Managers to explain technical trade-offs and their impact on outcomes
- Guide Developers through implementation decisions and review technical designs
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability, environments, and non-functional requirements

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business and stakeholder needs into clear, testable requirements and process models. They improve shared understanding before and during delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and requirements
- Refine backlog items and acceptance criteria
- Identify assumptions, dependencies, and gaps in requirements
- Maintain traceability between needs, delivered work, and validation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to refine priorities, outcomes, and acceptance criteria
- Support Project Managers with scope clarification, dependencies, and status information
- Collaborate with Developers to resolve requirement questions during implementation
- Partner with QA/Testing to ensure scenarios and tests reflect agreed requirements
- Confirm stakeholder understanding and capture decisions for the project record

---

## Release and DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Engineers make delivery repeatable and operationally safe by supporting environments, automation, observability, and rollback readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines, environments, and release automation
- Define deployment, rollback, and recovery procedures
- Support monitoring, alerting, and post-deployment verification
- Identify operational dependencies and release risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release plans, readiness, risks, and dependencies
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to automate builds, deployments, and operational checks
- Partner with QA/Testing to provide suitable test environments and smoke-test support
- Advise Product Managers and Stakeholders about operational readiness and release impact

---

## Security and Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners help ensure that project decisions protect information, users, and the organization. They provide guidance and review for applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Assess threats, data handling, and control gaps
- Recommend and track mitigations
- Participate in security validation and release-readiness reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and Business Analysts to make security and privacy requirements explicit
- Advise Technical Leads and Developers on controls, architecture, and secure implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy test coverage
- Report unresolved risks to Project Managers and Stakeholders through the agreed escalation path

---

## Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and production experience into planning and delivery. They help the team prepare for adoption, support, and incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, support trends, and operational constraints
- Define support, training, and runbook needs
- Participate in operational-readiness and incident-readiness reviews
- Monitor post-release feedback and help assess customer impact

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to turn customer feedback into backlog insight and priorities
- Coordinate with Project Managers on operational risks, communications, and readiness actions
- Partner with Release and DevOps Engineers on monitoring, runbooks, and post-release verification
- Collaborate with Developers and QA/Testing to reproduce issues and validate fixes

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, organizational support, and timely decisions when issues exceed the delivery team's authority or capacity.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and continued sponsorship
- Resolve escalated priority, funding, or cross-team decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and support resource alignment
- Review milestone outcomes, significant risks, and decisions requiring executive attention

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Receive regular status, risk, and decision updates from Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on expected outcomes, priority, and business value
- Support escalation decisions involving Stakeholders and dependent teams
- Allow Developers, QA/Testing, and other specialists to retain ownership of their delivery responsibilities while providing organizational support
